# Why the market is attractive (and why Poland isn't the target)

## The market

- **300M motorcycle riders** globally (Statista); ~10–15M active touring riders
- Motorcycle social/community app TAM: **$50M–$100M** (conservative)
- Event discovery TAM: **$20M–$50M** (niche but growing)
- Adjacent event ticketing market: **$70B+** — we sit at the intersection
- Existing players (REVER, TONIT, MOTOSPOT, BikerOrNot) are either tracking-only, forum-only, or US-only with outdated UX. Nobody owns the full stack.

## The money

- **Freemium model:** free core (routes, crews, map, events), premium subscription ($20–40/year) for advanced features
- **Year 1 SOM:** ~$2M (100K users × 10% premium conversion × $20/year) — buddy finder side alone
- **Events side:** commission on paid event tickets, sponsored event placement
- **Turf-war mechanics** create retention loops that generic event platforms can't match — riders come back to defend their crew's districts

## Why not Poland

- **Seasonality kills it.** Poland's riding season is May–October at best. November–April is dead. A social app that goes dark for 5 months loses its network effect.
- **Target markets:** year-round riding climates — southern US (California, Florida, Texas), Mediterranean Europe (Italy, Spain, Greece), Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia), Brazil
- **Poland is the demo, not the market.** We built here because it's home and the hackathon is here — but the architecture (region-agnostic, voivodeship → state/province swap) is designed for expansion from day one.
