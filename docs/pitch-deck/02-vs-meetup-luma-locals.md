# What makes ironCult different

## Meetup and Luma are for everyone. We're for riders.

- **Meetup** is a general-purpose event platform. You'll find knitting circles and coding bootcamps next to (maybe) one motorcycle night. No ride-specific filtering, no crew identity, no map.
- **Luma** is a sleek event-ticketing tool. Great for organizers, useless for a rider who wants to know "who's riding near me right now?"
- **Facebook Events** are noisy, not motorcycle-filtered, and algorithmically buried.

## ironCult is what Locals should have been

**Locals** (shut down recently) tried to be a hyperlocal social layer — but it was platform-agnostic, unfocused, and couldn't hold a niche. We're betting the opposite: **own one community deeply** rather than every community shallowly.

- Riders don't need another generic social app. They need a place that speaks their language: crews, routes, turf, ride types, bike nights.
- The live map with real-time presence and crew turf-war is something no general event platform can replicate — it's motorcycle-native by design.
- Buddy finder matches by region + date + riding style + pace — not a swipe deck, a purpose-built tool for "anyone riding in Mazowieckie this Saturday?"

## The gap

No app combines crew identity, route logging, live presence, event discovery, and territorial competition in one place. REVER tracks friends. TONIT has communities. BikerOrNot is a forum. Nobody does all of it — and nobody makes it feel like a game.
