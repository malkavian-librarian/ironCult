# [MR] Motorcycle Touring Buddy Finder — Market Research

## 1. Idea Summary

* **Description:** Tinder-for-motorcycle-trips: input planned route + dates → matched with riders doing similar trips. Filter by riding style, experience level, pace, language.
* **Target:** Solo motorcycle touring riders wanting companions
* **Category:** Social/matching app for motorcycle travel

## 2. Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)

**TAM:** ~$50M-$100M (motorcycle social/community app market)

* Global motorcycle riders: ~300M (Statista)
* Active touring community: ~10-15M riders globally
* Premium subscription: $20-40/year → TAM $200M-$600M (optimistic), $50M-$100M (conservative)

**SAM:** ~$30M (touring riders actively seeking riding partners, ~30% of TAM)

**SOM:** ~$2M Year 1 (freemium model, 100K users × $20/year premium conversion at 10%)

## 3. Competitive Analysis

| Competitor | Features | Pricing | Weaknesses |
| -- | -- | -- | -- |
| REVER | "Never ride alone" live tracking | Freemium ($39.99/yr) | No trip matching, just tracking existing friends |
| BikerOrNot | Social network for bikers | Free | No trip matching, outdated platform |
| TONIT | Community, ride tracking | Free + IAP | No route-based matching |
| MOTOSPOT | Social, events, ride planning | Free + IAP | No buddy matching algorithm |
| Facebook groups | Group ride coordination | Free | Chaotic, not location/time-aware, no matching |

## 4. Market Gap

No app matches riders by planned route + dates + riding style + experience level. Existing platforms either track existing friends (REVER) or are general social networks (BikerOrNot, TONIT). The trip-matching use case is completely unaddressed.

## 5. Reddit VoC

* Recurring "anyone riding in [region]?" posts across r/motorcycles, r/motocamping, r/advrider
* "Is there a cool motorcycle app that I can freely use?" — users looking for community features (r/motorcycles)
* "Never ride alone again" is a strong desire signal
